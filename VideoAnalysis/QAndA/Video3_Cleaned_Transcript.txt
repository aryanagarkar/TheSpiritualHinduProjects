**Question:** What's the difference between weak emergence and strong emergence?

**Answer:** I think that emergence in general is basically just the measure of surprise for the observer. A phenomenon is considered emergent by us if whatever it's doing was something that we didn't anticipate. So, it's relative. It's just a measure of the amount of surprise, how much extra is the system doing that you, knowing the rules and the various properties of its parts, didn't see coming. That's emergence.
