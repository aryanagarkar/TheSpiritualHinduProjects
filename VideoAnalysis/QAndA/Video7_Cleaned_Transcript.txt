**Question:** What is the significance of the term "qubit" and how did it come about?
**Answer:** A qubit is a quantum bit, a fundamental unit of quantum information. The term is a portmanteau of quantum and bit. The term bit goes back to Claude Shannon and is a portmanteau of binary digits. The name qubit, spelled Q-U-B-I-T, was coined by Ben Schumacher at Kenyon College and Bill Wooters at Williams College. The spelling was inspired by a unit of measure in the Bible called a cubit, spelled C-U-B-I-T. They replaced the C with a Q to signify a modern version of a cubit.

**Answer:** The Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen paper from 1935 is an extended philosophical argument that lays out a metaphysical claim about quantum mechanics. **Question:** What is decoherence and how did it come about?
